About

简介

I am currently studying Computer Science and Technology at Shandong University. Alongside my background in computer systems and software development, I am increasingly interested in applying machine learning to musical understanding and generation. I enjoy exploring the connections between musical structure, temporal representation, generative models, and real-time interaction.

Outside research, I am an amateur pianist with a strong interest in classical music, especially the works of J. S. Bach. This musical experience motivates my curiosity about how computational models can represent musical structure and expressive performance.

Research Interests

研究兴趣

Generative Music Modeling

Deep generative models for symbolic and audio music generation, with an emphasis on controllability and musical coherence.

Real-time Music Accompaniment

Interactive accompaniment generation conditioned on live vocal or instrumental input, considering temporal alignment and responsiveness.

Music Representation Learning

Learning representations of musical structure, harmony, texture, and performance for downstream understanding and generation.

Acoustic World Models

Temporal modeling and future-state prediction in acoustic latent spaces, and their potential role in interactive music generation.

Expressive Music Performance

Modeling expressive performance, including performance-style analysis, expressive rendering, and localized performance editing.

Multimodal Learning

Exploring relationships across audio, symbolic music, language, and other modalities.
